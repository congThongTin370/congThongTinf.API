# Requirements

## Admin

Admin can:

- View dashboard/statistics
- Create/manage accounts
- Assign roles
- Upload/manage internal documents

## Journalist

Journalist can:

- Create news
- Edit news
- Publish news
- Manage their own articles

## News

A news article contains:

- Title
- Summary
- Content
- Cover image
- Category
- Author
- Status
- PublishedAt
- CreatedAt
- UpdatedAt

Possible statuses:

- Draft
- Pending
- Published
- Archived
